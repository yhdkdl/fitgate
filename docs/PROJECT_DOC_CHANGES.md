# FitGate — Project Scope Decisions & Modifications

_Date: October 25, 2026_
_Purpose: A dated record of every scope decision, modification, and addition made since the original project document, formatted for updating the university project document._

---

## 1. Deferred to Future Work

- **Weekly member check-in, trainer check-in notification, and Monday reminder (November 1, 2026):** Deferred to a future release; the weekly member check-in form (energy, recovery, motivation ratings 1–5), the trainer notification upon check-in submission, the scheduled Monday reminder, and dynamic check-in intensity adjustments are deferred. Intensity is determined deterministically by phase and fitness level, and trainers adjust intensity directly by editing the draft before approval. The rules engine's adaptive intensity strategy code is preserved ready for future check-in integration.
- **Blood pressure, heart-rate zones, and posture screening checklist (October 30, 2026):** Deferred to a future release; standard physical circumferences, body composition metrics, and resting heart rate cover baseline trainer assessment needs without requiring specialized clinical diagnostics or complex postural assessment tools.
- **Trainer availability schedule (Sprint 18):** Deferred to a future release; gym trainers coordinate availability directly with gym management in v1, reducing scheduling interface complexity.
- **Trainer-to-member direct in-app messaging (Sprint 19):** Deferred to a future release; trainer-client communication takes place in person or via standard communication channels, avoiding the overhead of real-time messaging on shared hosting.
- **Group class scheduling and booking (Sprint 26):** Deferred to a future release; target pilot gyms operate primarily on open floor and general access memberships rather than structured group studio bookings.
- **Milestone badges and member progress charts (Sprint 28a):** Deferred to a future release; gamification badges and historical chart visualizations deferred to prioritize core membership, attendance, and payment workflows.
- **Advanced analytics (peak-hours heatmap, cohort retention, referral analytics):** Deferred to a future release; heavy analytical aggregations deferred to preserve performance on shared cPanel hosting; revenue by plan is retained as the sole premium analytics metric.

## 2. Changed

- **Notice Board and Telegram Channel Bridge (November 2, 2026):**
  - **In-App Notice Board (Sprint 28):** Gym Owner and Manager post notices (title 2–100 characters, body 5–1,000 characters). Members of that gym see the latest notices on their Home screen and on a dedicated Notices screen; users from other gyms cannot view them. Does NOT create one notification row per member, avoiding database bloat.
  - **Telegram Channel Bridge:** Optional broadcast to the gym's public Telegram channel. The Owner enters only the channel name (format `@name`, 5 to 32 letters, digits, or underscores starting with a letter) in Gym Settings, with a "Send test message" button. The platform bot token is read from the `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN` environment variable.
  - **Resilient Fallback & Development Mock:** In development and test environments, a mock sender records deliveries without calling external network APIs. If the token is missing, Telegram is not configured, or a send fails, the in-app notice still succeeds, and staff see an informational "not sent to Telegram" notice. No member Telegram IDs are collected. Staging verification is documented in `docs/MANUAL_STEPS.md`.

- **Equipment Registry Rules, Completeness Setting, Purchase Prices, and Member Visibility (November 2, 2026):**
  - **Registry Completeness Setting:** Added "My equipment list is complete" (`is_equipment_list_complete`, default False) in Gym Settings. In plan generation, Mode 1 uses profile equipment when registry is empty; Mode 2 removes unusable items (`under_maintenance`, `out_of_service`, or quantity 0); Mode 3 intersects profile equipment with registered usable items (`good`, `needs_maintenance`, quantity > 0), always preserving bodyweight exercises. The Training Plan Review displays `Equipment source: ...`.
  - **Purchase Price Tracking:** Added optional unit purchase price (`unit_purchase_price_etb`, 0.01 to 10,000,000 ETB, 2 decimals) on equipment items. The Equipment screen displays "Total investment" = sum of price x quantity for Owner and Manager, with a note indicating how many items have no price recorded. Prices and total investment are strictly hidden from Trainer, Reception, and Member roles. Price changes are audit-logged (`equipment_price_updated`).
  - **Member Equipment Visibility:** Members see only usable equipment unless the owner enables "Members see unavailable equipment". Serial numbers, maintenance logs, prices, and minimum stock alerts are strictly stripped for members.
  - **Equipment & Expense Integration (Sprint 29a):** When adding or editing an equipment item with a purchase price, the UI offers a checkbox "Also record as an expense". When selected, an expense entry of category `equipment` is linked to the equipment item (`equipment_id`), ensuring the purchase is reflected in financial reporting without double counting.

- **Plan Upgrade Entitlement Enforcement and Subscription Card Enhancements (November 2, 2026):**
  - **Upgrade Entitlement Matching:** An active subscription switch counts as an upgrade only if the new plan price is strictly higher AND includes all entitlements of the member's current active plan. Specifically, if the current plan is trainer-inclusive (`trainer_inclusive=True`), the new plan must also be trainer-inclusive (`trainer_inclusive=True`). If a trainer-inclusive member selects a non-trainer plan, the upgrade is refused across all payment paths (Cash, Bank Transfer, and Chapa via the single shared quote calculation) with message: `"This plan does not include a trainer. You can choose it when your current plan ends on <date>."` On the member upgrade screen and staff desk payment screen, such plans are shown disabled with that explanation.
  - **Renewal Entitlement Warning:** When renewing after a plan has ended (or when current plan has expired), choosing a non-trainer plan is allowed, but the staff desk payment screen and member checkout display an informational warning: `"This plan has no trainer. The member will lose their trainer's AI plans."` Existing trainer assignments and historical approved plans are retained, but future AI plan generation is inaccessible without an active trainer plan.
  - **Member Subscription Card Enhancements:** Member Home (`/dashboard`) and My membership (`/membership`) subscription cards display plan name, "Trainer included: Yes/No", status, start date, end date, "X days left" with progress bar (matching upgrade quote days-remaining math), plan duration, queued following plan ("Next plan: <name> starts on <date>"), "Frozen until <date>" when frozen, and "Waiting for payment" when pending.

- **Increment 5 Equipment, Analytics, Communication scope decisions (November 1, 2026):**
  - **Equipment Registry & Maintenance (Sprint 25):** Equipment category is REQUIRED and chosen from the rules engine's equipment catalog (`barbell`, `dumbbells`, `cable_machine`, `resistance_bands`, `kettlebells`, `pull_up_bar`, `bench`, `leg_press`, `squat_rack`, `cardio_machines`, `bodyweight`, `other`). Name is free text 2 to 100 characters (letters, digits, spaces, hyphen, apostrophe, period). Quantity a whole number from 0 to 10,000; minimum quantity optional whole number (0 to 10,000) triggering low-quantity alerts when quantity <= min quantity. Purchase date optional, not in the future. Reporting an issue uses a predefined list of reasons (`broken`, `worn_out`, `noisy_unsafe`, `other` with short text up to 200 characters), with no free text otherwise. MaintenanceLog tracks issue reports and condition transitions. Owner/Manager update condition and log resolutions. Member visibility of unavailable items controlled by gym config (default off).
  - **AI Equipment Wiring:** Usable equipment for plan generation is determined by the gym's equipment registry items whose condition is `good` or `needs_maintenance` and quantity > 0, mapped through category and intersected with the member's profile equipment access. Items `under_maintenance` or `out_of_service` (or quantity 0) are excluded. If the gym has no registry items at all, gym-side filtering is OFF (member's profile access alone decides). Replaces the previous stand-in `unavailable_equipment` parameter with the registry query.
  - **Dashboard Analytics (Sprint 27):** Owner and Manager only (scoped to requesting gym; Super Admin, Reception, Trainer, Member denied 403). Core metrics: active members (derived status `active`, frozen shown separately), today's check-ins (count in `Africa/Addis_Ababa` day), monthly revenue (confirmed net cash received this calendar month, upgrade net amount counted, reversed excluded), memberships expiring within 7 days (active subscriptions ending in <= 7 days with no queued following subscription). Superseded subscriptions are excluded. Premium analytics: revenue by plan (net cash received per plan), Growth and Pro only (`has_analytics=True`), Starter sees upgrade prompt. Timing check executes in under 3 seconds against a seeded 1,000-member, 1-year attendance dataset.
  - **Notice Board & Telegram Bridge (Sprint 28):** Owner and Manager post notices (title up to 100 chars, body up to 1,000 chars) displayed on member Home and Notices screens scoped to the gym. Avoids creating individual notification rows per member. Optional Telegram channel broadcast configured in Gym Settings (@name, 5–32 chars) with "Send test message" button. Uses platform bot token `TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN`. If unconfigured, missing, or send fails, in-app notice succeeds with a short note to the owner. Development uses mock sender; staging verification documented in `docs/MANUAL_STEPS.md`.
  - **CSV Export (Sprint 29):** Owner and Manager only, scoped to gym, audit-logged with export type and row count. Exports: members (name, phone, email, status, plan, start/end dates; strictly NO health/photo/measurement data), payments (receipt, date, method, plan, gross, credit, net received, upgrade yes/no, status, bank ref, confirmed by), attendance (date/time, member, method). Protects against spreadsheet formula injection (leading apostrophe for cells starting with `=`, `+`, `-`, `@`). Excel-friendly UTF-8 (BOM). Optional date range up to 1 year.
  - **Expenses and Financial Reports (Sprint 29a):** Owner and Manager log expenses: amount > 0 up to 10,000,000 ETB, category from predefined list (`rent`, `salaries`, `utilities`, `equipment`, `maintenance`, `marketing`, `supplies`, `other`), optional note up to 500 chars, date not in future. Mistaken entries are voided (kept in DB, excluded from totals, audit-logged; no hard delete). Reports include revenue vs. expenses (confirmed net revenue minus expenses), attendance summary, and revenue breakdown, all scoped to the gym.

- **AI generation reliability, shared choice lists, and lightweight failure logging (November 1, 2026):**
  - **Shared Choice Lists:** Frontend screens and backend validators unified on ONE shared definition for all five list fields: weekly focus (`no_focus`, `legs`, `upper_body`, `core`, `cardio`, `mobility`, `other`), health limitations (15 standard codes + other), equipment access (8 equipment categories + other), dietary notes (11 restrictions + other), and rejection reasons (`too_hard`, `too_easy`, `exercises_not_suitable`, `equipment_not_available`, `nutrition_not_suitable`, `other` with 5–200 char note). Removed unagreed "Wrong Phase" code from frontend.
  - **Lenient Output Parsing & Strict Safety:** Strips markdown code blocks and any surrounding explanatory text from AI JSON. Accepts numbers where text is expected and text where numbers are expected (`sets`, `reps`, `rpe`, `rest_seconds`, `session_length`), filling missing optional fields (`notes`, `rest_seconds`) with sensible defaults. Strictly rejects and counts as failure: incorrect session count, empty exercise list, any exercise or equipment on member exclusion list, missing nutrition, or numeric calorie targets.
  - **Fallback Ordering & Retry:** Ordered fallback chain prioritizes measured working providers (Google AI Studio Gemini Flash when key present, followed by best OpenRouter models, removing broken/rate-limited models). Retries the same model once after 2 seconds on HTTP 429 rate limits before falling back.
  - **Failure Logging Refactored:** Removed per-attempt and per-failure audit log entries and owner notifications. Audit log entries are recorded only on successful draft creation, edit, approval, and rejection. Failures increment a compact daily counter (`AIDailyFailureCounter`) per model per reason per day (counts only, zero PII), pruned after 30 days by the nightly maintenance command. If all attempts fail in a generation request, at most ONE in-app notification per gym per day is delivered to the Owner ("Plan generation had problems today. Trainers can retry later.").
- **Plan upgrade with credit moved from deferred to active in Sprint 12c (November 1, 2026):** Moved "mid-subscription plan changes" out of SPEC §10's deferred features. Built as Sprint 12c ("Plan upgrade with credit"): active members can upgrade to a higher-priced plan paying the prorated credit difference calculated strictly on the server (`credit = net_paid * remaining_days / total_days`, rounded down to 2 decimals). Old subscription transitions to `superseded` and is excluded from attendance, check-ins, expiry sweep, and reminders. New subscription starts immediately for full duration; trainer assignment triggers on trainer-inclusive plans. Payment and sequential receipt record gross, credit, and net cash received. Downgrades/equal-price changes are refused ("This plan costs less than your current one. It can start when your current plan ends on <date>."), frozen/suspended/pending members are blocked until resolved, cash refunds are barred (net <= 0 refused), and upgrade payments cannot be reversed via bank transfer reversal. In-flight Chapa state changes flag the payment for Owner resolution without losing member funds.
- **Trimmed Sprint 23 and 24 scope for Increment 4 closeout (November 1, 2026):** Sprints 23 and 24 focus on core approval actions and trainer oversight. Plan approval triggers one in-app notification to the member ("Your training plan for the week of <date> is ready."). The member dashboard renders a "Waiting for your trainer" card when an assigned client on a trainer-inclusive plan has no approved plan yet (unapproved drafts are never shown or hinted at). The Trainer Overview features a "Clients with no approved plan this week" card displaying the count and list of the trainer's own clients lacking an approved plan for the current Monday-anchored week, with status badges ("No draft yet" vs. "Draft waiting for your review") linking to their plan screen. Weekly check-in forms and cron reminders are deferred.
- **AI plan generation error handling, Google AI Studio fallback, and DOB validation (October 31, 2026):** All-provider generation failures return a clean service-unavailable response ("Generation failed, try again later") on the Generate plan screen, trigger in-app notifications and audit logs for Owner and Manager, and do not consume the gym's 20/hour quota. Added optional Google AI Studio (`GEMINI_API_KEY`) first in the fallback chain. Generation strictly refuses members with missing or invalid date of birth with "Member date of birth is missing." Removed the 500-character free-text trainer note from initial generation in favor of structured weekly focus options.
- **Selectable list codes for Fitness Profile, medical caution notice, focus multi-select, and rejection reasons checklist (October 30, 2026):** Replaced free-text entry for health limitations, equipment access, and dietary notes with required multi-select lists of standard codes plus optional 'other' text (max 200 characters). 'None' and 'No restrictions' enforce mutual exclusivity. A medical caution notice ("Confirm with the member's doctor; review plans for this member extra carefully") displays on profile entry and plan review when cardiovascular, metabolic, respiratory, pregnancy, or surgical conditions are present. The rules engine uses limitation and equipment codes for deterministic exercise exclusions. Weekly trainer note is replaced with 'Focus this week' multi-select (`no_focus`, `legs`, `upper_body`, `core`, `cardio`, `mobility`, `other`), and plan rejection requires ticking at least one reason (`too_hard`, `too_easy`, `exercises_not_suitable`, `equipment_not_available`, `nutrition_not_suitable`, `other` + 5–200 char text).
- **Fitness profile entered exclusively by assigned trainer (October 30, 2026):** Replaced member/reception intake during registration. The assigned trainer enters the profile on the client's page during the first visit (in a "Fitness profile" section next to measurements). Owner and Manager can view and edit; member sees it read-only on their own page; unrelated trainers, Reception, Super Admin, and other gyms receive 403 Forbidden. Registration screens remain unchanged. Fields: goal, fitness level, health limitations, equipment access, training days per week (2–6), session length (30, 45, 60, 90 mins), dietary notes (max 1000 chars: allergies, fasting periods). Height, weight, body fat %, lean body mass, and resting heart rate are populated from latest trainer-recorded measurements and are never typed again.
- **Weekly plan structure and Monday-anchored scheduling (October 30, 2026):** Plans cover one week (`week_start_date` on Monday) with sessions named Session A, Session B, etc. (one per training day, not tied to weekdays). Mid-week joiners receive a plan for the current week immediately with default intensity for the phase. General Ethiopian nutrition guidance is included respecting fasting periods without calorie precision.
- **AI plan generation workflow, 3-draft weekly limit, and hourly gym quota (October 30, 2026):** The assigned trainer or Owner/Manager triggers plan generation on the member's page or plan review screen (members and Reception denied 403). At most 3 drafts per member per plan week (initial draft + 2 regenerations). The third draft cannot be rejected: it can only be directly edited and approved ("Edit this draft yourself"). Only one open pending draft per member per week. A gym-wide limit of 20 generations per hour protects free AI quotas, replacing any daily per-member limit.
- **Trainer review workflow and audit logging (October 30, 2026):** The trainer directly edits drafts (exercises and nutrition) before approving, or rejects with a reason (5–500 chars) which triggers a regeneration incorporating the note. Editing, approval, and rejection are audit-logged (including `was_edited` flag). Members see only approved plans: current week's approved plan, previous approved plan labelled with week if new week is pending, or "Waiting for your trainer".
- **Decoupled weekly check-in (October 30, 2026):** Monday check-in form (energy, recovery, motivation, 1–5; duplicates refused) notifies the assigned trainer and feeds intensity adjustment for the next requested plan, rather than auto-generating plans. Monday evening reminder command runs if check-in is missed.
- **Strict PII redaction and prompt safety (October 30, 2026):** Personal identifiers (name, phone, email, member id) are completely stripped from AI provider calls. Only age, gender, goal, fitness level, training days, session length, equipment, limitations, dietary notes, trainer note, and latest measurements are sent as plain data. Provider output must match a fixed plan structure or count as a failed attempt. Three failures in a request notifies the owner, logs the failure, and leaves other operations unaffected.
- **Trainer capacity configuration range and active client safety guard (October 30, 2026):** Gym Owner and Manager can configure a trainer's maximum client capacity between 1 and 100 (narrowed from 200). Lowering capacity below the trainer's current number of active clients is refused with an instruction to reassign clients first, preventing accidental orphan states. The change is audit-logged with previous and new values.
- **Strict trainer client scoping on server for measurements and photos (October 30, 2026):** Trainers are restricted on the server to view, search, record measurements for, and access profile photos of only gym members currently assigned to them. Access attempts for non-assigned members, former clients, members of other gyms, or standard plan members are denied with HTTP 403.
- **Revenue by plan designated as the sole premium analytics feature:** In this version, the premium analytics module (Growth and Pro tiers) includes only revenue by plan; heatmap, cohort retention, and referral analytics are deferred, while the basic dashboard remains available on all tiers.
- **Subscription tier structure restated with Pro hidden until release:** Starter provides core operations (registration, plans, payments, QR check-in, notice board, basic dashboard, audit logs); Growth adds the trainer module, AI plans, membership freeze, and revenue by plan; Pro adds multi-branch. Pro is hidden from the landing page and Create Gym forms until multi-branch ships.
- **Multi-branch management (Increment 6) reordered to an optional final phase:** Multi-branch is scheduled after Increment 7 as an optional stretch increment (decision date set by project owner), ensuring the core single-branch product deploys cleanly first.
- **Body measurements recorded exclusively by Trainers (Sprint 7b):** Removed member self-reported measurements during registration; measurements (height, weight, circumferences) are captured exclusively by professional trainers to guarantee data integrity for BMI, body composition, and AI planning.
- **Assisted onboarding replaces open gym self-registration (Sprints 4, 4a):** Prospective gyms submit a "Request a Demo" inquiry and pay platform fees via bank transfer; Super Admin provisions the gym and owner account, avoiding unpaid or unverified tenant sprawl.
- **Database compatibility adjusted for PostgreSQL 13:** Adapted production deployment settings and verified test suites against Yegara cPanel's managed PostgreSQL 13.23 environment, replacing the earlier unmanaged VPS/Docker requirement.
- **Member status model gains a fifth `pending` state:** New member registrations without an active subscription start as `pending`; `MemberSubscription` acts as the single source of truth, and check-in verifies subscription validity directly rather than trusting static status flags.
- **Owner freeze days allowed setting capped by Super Admin entitlement cap (Item 12):** `GymConfig.freeze_days_allowed` is configurable by gym Owner up to `freeze_days_cap`, which is controlled exclusively by Super Admin.
- **Member registration requires binary gender selection Female or Male (Item 13):** Replaced non-binary and optional gender choices with mandatory Female or Male selection.
- **Member self-profile lockdown restricts member accounts to password changes only (Item 14):** Members cannot self-edit name, phone, email, or photo, which are managed exclusively by gym staff.

## 3. Added

- **Trainer profile photo and member "My trainer" card (October 30, 2026):** Trainers can upload and replace an optional profile photo via account settings (JPEG, PNG, WebP up to 2 MB), visible to gym Owner/Manager on the Trainers management screen. Members on trainer-inclusive plans see a "My trainer" card on Home and Membership screens with trainer photo, name, and specialization (showing initials if no photo, "Trainer not assigned yet" if unassigned, and hidden on standard plans). Private photo routes strictly enforce that members can only view their own current assigned trainer's photo.
- **Extended body measurement fields and automated derived composition metrics (October 30, 2026):** Added optional circumference fields (chest 50–200 cm, bicep L/R 15–80 cm, thigh L/R 30–120 cm, calf L/R 20–80 cm) and vitals (resting heart rate 30–220 bpm integer, trainer-measured body fat % 2–70%) stored within flexible measurements data without schema changes. Automatically derives waist-to-hip ratio (waist / hip) and lean body mass (`weight * (1 - body fat % / 100)`, labeled "measured" when trainer-measured body fat % is present or "estimate" via formula).
- **Latest measurement entry correction workflow (October 30, 2026):** The assigned trainer can correct errors on only the latest measurement entry for their client. The correction is audit-logged with before-and-after values and flagged as "corrected" in history, while older historical records remain permanently immutable.
- **Credential-agnostic check-in pipeline (v1 QR only):** Unified check-in engine routes all entrance attempts through one internal pipeline with attendance method tracking (`qr` or `manual_override`), laying the architectural foundation for future signed NFC tags or biometric hardware without altering business logic.
- **Notice board Telegram channel bridge (Sprint 28):** Gym announcements can optionally broadcast to the gym's public Telegram channel via a platform bot, matching common Ethiopian member communication practices without collecting private member contact IDs.
- **Dedicated entrance kiosk scanner and live check-in monitoring (Sprints 8b, 8c):** Added a full-screen, hands-free tablet camera scanner for front-desk entry and a live ~5-second polling attendance feed on staff dashboards, replacing manual desk check-in registers.
- **Added: bank transfer as a member payment method, with reconciliation and reversal (Sprint 12b):** Direct bank transfer enabled for members at front desk (Owner, Manager, Reception) with unique bank reference enforcement, Owner reconciliation workflow, 7-day unattended flagging cron, and immutable reversal with previous subscription state restoration.
- **Staff-assisted member registration supports atomic upfront plan payment (Item 16):** Staff can optionally collect cash or bank transfer payment and activate a plan during walk-in registration in a single atomic transaction.
- **Public gym payment configuration status endpoint exposed for unauthenticated client discovery (Item 17):** Public endpoint exposes payment acceptance flags without authentication or credentials to support registration and checkout workflows.
- **Bank transfer payments support payment proof screenshot upload (Item 21):** Staff and members can upload transaction confirmation screenshots for bank transfer payments, stored securely in tenant storage for owner reconciliation.

- **Staff early unfreeze workflow (October 30, 2026):** Gym staff (Owner, Manager, Reception) can end a member's active or approved future freeze early. For active freezes, actual days used are calculated, unused days are subtracted from the subscription end date and returned to the member's annual allowance, and member status is reactivated immediately with instant QR pass re-enablement. For unstarted approved freezes, the freeze is cancelled completely with all extended days removed. The action is row-locked (`select_for_update`), idempotent, audit-logged, and dispatches an in-app notification to the member. Members, trainers, and Super Admin are refused on the server.
- **Gym configuration and freeze cap controls (October 30, 2026):** Super Admin Configure Gym screen includes inputs for freeze cap (`freeze_days_cap`, whole number 0–365, default 30), payment grace days (default 7), and subscription reminder days (default [14, 7]). Owner Gym Settings screen displays freeze days allowed (0 up to the gym cap, with values exceeding cap rejected) and member reminder days, while Starter tier gyms are strictly blocked from using membership freeze. The member's freeze request screen reflects the gym's real configured allowance and remaining days.
- **Equipment registry completion setting, 3 plan generation equipment modes, price tracking, and member view permissions (November 2, 2026):**
  - Owner setting "My equipment list is complete" (`is_equipment_list_complete`, default off) in Gym Settings.
  - Three plan generation equipment modes:
    1. Registry empty: uses member profile equipment only (`member profile only`).
    2. Registry not empty and setting off: uses member profile equipment minus unusable equipment (under maintenance or out of service) (`gym registry (unusable items removed)`).
    3. Setting on: uses member profile equipment intersected with registered usable items (good / needs maintenance, quantity > 0, matched by category); bodyweight is always allowed (`gym registry (complete list)`).
  - Plan review screen displays: `Equipment source: member profile only | gym registry (unusable items removed) | gym registry (complete list)` (Trainer, Owner, Manager).
  - Equipment registry screen displays note + `Categories registered: x of y`.
  - Equipment price: optional unit purchase price (ETB, > 0, up to 10,000,000.00, 2 decimals). Equipment screen displays `Total investment` = sum of price x quantity, with note `N items have no price`. Only Owner and Manager can view or edit prices; Trainer, Reception, and Member never see prices or total investment. Price edits are audit-logged (`equipment_price_updated`).
  - Member equipment card respects Owner setting `Members see unavailable equipment` (default off). When off, members see only usable equipment. Members never see maintenance notes, serial numbers, or prices.
